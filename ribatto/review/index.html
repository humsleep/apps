Sample footage for App Review

This page exists so a reviewer has a match video to test Ribatto with. There is nothing here for the general public.

1. The match video to import

match-sample.mp4 — 4 minutes, 45 MB, 720p.
A doubles tennis match filmed from behind the baseline. It contains two rallies with long gaps between them, which is what the app is for.

On an iPhone: tap the link, then Save to Photos when it finishes. In Ribatto tap + at the top of the Library, choose From Photos, pick this video and choose Tennis when asked which sport. Detection runs on its own and finds two rallies in well under a minute.

2. What the free pass changes

The pass is opened by watching a rewarded video ad. Google AdMob does not serve ads to an app that is not yet published on the App Store, so on a pre-release build that button reports that no ad can be loaded and the pass cannot be reached. These two files are the same rally exported both ways, so the difference can be judged without it.

  • rally-free.mp4 — without a pass: 720p, watermarked, the source's own framing.
  • rally-pass.mp4 — with a pass: full quality, no watermark, and re-framed vertically for Reels and Shorts, following where the rally was actually played rather than cutting down the middle.

The pass changes export quality and framing only. Detection, trimming, adding or splitting rallies, captions, the season figures and reel building are all available without it, and there are no in-app purchases in this build.

Notes

The footage is the developer's own recreational match, shared with the consent of the people in it, and is used only for this purpose. The clip in section 1 is a short excerpt re-encoded to 720p to keep the download small; detection works from the sound of the racket strike, so the smaller picture does not change what the app finds.
